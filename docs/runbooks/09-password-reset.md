# 9. Reset a player's password

**Who:** an organizer of the player's LARP (or a superadministrator for any account).

The app does not send email. When a player forgets their password, you create a one-time link and give it to them.

1. In the admin panel, make sure the menu shows the player's LARP, then open **Players**.
2. In the player's row menu choose **Password reset link**, then **Create reset link**.
3. Copy the link and send it to the player (Discord, SMS, in person). It is shown only once.
4. The player opens the link, chooses a new password (at least 12 characters) and signs in.

The link works once and expires after 24 hours. Creating another link cancels the previous one. Changing the password signs the account out on every other device.

Organizers can reset only player accounts in their own LARPs, not organizer or superadministrator accounts. Superadministrators can also use **Password reset link** in **Accounts**.

Players who sign in only with Discord don't need a password; the link lets them add one.
